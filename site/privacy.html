Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

StickerDock is built to keep your stickers on your phone and in your control. We don't run user accounts, we don't sell data, and we don't track you across apps. This page explains exactly what happens to your information.

What stays on your phone

  • Your sticker library, packs, names, tags and favourites are stored in a local database on your device.
  • Your private vault never leaves the phone — it is never uploaded, synced or shared.
  • Reading the words on your stickers for search (OCR) and auto-tagging happens on the device. The images are not sent anywhere for this.
  • Backups you create are plain files saved where you choose; we don't receive them.

What the app reads from our servers

  • The Discover feed loads published sticker packs from our content backend (Supabase) using a public, read-only key. Browsing Discover does not require an account and does not identify you.
  • Standard technical information (such as your IP address and app version) is handled by our hosting provider to deliver those requests, as with any website. We don't use it to build a profile of you.

WhatsApp

When you add a pack to WhatsApp, Android hands your chosen stickers to WhatsApp through the official sticker interface. StickerDock does not read your chats, contacts or messages, and never sends on your behalf.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no login, no email or phone number required to use the app.
  • No advertising SDKs and no selling of personal data.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

StickerDock is a general-audience app. Content marked 18+ is off by default and kept in a separate lane behind a setting. We design against sexual content involving minors and remove reported material.

Content and takedowns

Discover packs are curated. If you believe a published sticker infringes your rights or shouldn't be there, email us (below) with the pack and a short description and we'll review and remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above and note it in the app's release notes.
