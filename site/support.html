Help & support

Quick answers to the things people ask most.

Add a pack to WhatsApp

Open a pack in the StickerDock app and tap Add to WhatsApp. Choose your WhatsApp (personal or Business) when asked. The pack appears in WhatsApp's sticker tray, ready to send.

Use both your WhatsApps

StickerDock keeps one library and adds your packs to every WhatsApp on the phone — the main app, WhatsApp Business, and a Dual-Messenger/cloned WhatsApp. You don't maintain stickers twice.

A cloned WhatsApp (Samsung Dual Messenger / second space) needs a one-time setup from the app's companion screen. The copy there has no library of its own — it serves that profile from your main library.

The private vault

The vault is for stickers you want to keep to yourself. Vault stickers stay on the phone, never sync, and never appear in Discover. Turn the vault on in Settings; it asks for confirmation before opening.

18+ content

Adult-humour stickers are off by default. Turn them on in Settings if you want them in Discover and your library. There is no sexual content, and nothing involving minors is ever in the adult lane.

Back up and restore

Settings → Backup saves your whole library — stickers, packs, names and tags — as a single file (the vault is left out). Restore from that file on the same or a new phone. Keep the file somewhere safe.

Make your own sticker

Pick a photo, let StickerDock cut out the subject, add a caption (Hinglish welcome), and save it to a pack.

Troubleshooting

  • A pack didn't show in WhatsApp: reopen WhatsApp's sticker tray; if it's still missing, remove and re-add the pack from StickerDock.
  • Discover looks empty: new packs are published through the day — pull to refresh, or check back.
  • Search isn't finding a sticker: give it a moment after import; the phone reads the words on your stickers in the background so search can find them by text.
