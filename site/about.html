About StickerDock

The India-first WhatsApp sticker app: one tasteful, always-fresh library that works across both your WhatsApps — made for how India actually chats.

Why we built it

Stickers are how a lot of India talks. But the options are noisy: watermarked packs, English-only humour, and apps that forget you have two WhatsApps. StickerDock is the opposite — curated Hinglish and Bollywood reactions, no ads, no watermarks, and one library that serves your personal and Business WhatsApp alike.

What makes it different

  • Made for India. Hinglish captions and Bollywood moments, not generic imports.
  • Fresh every day. New reaction and meme packs land daily, picked for what people are sending.
  • Two WhatsApps, one library. Personal, Business and cloned WhatsApp — all from one place.
  • Private by design. Your library lives on your phone. A vault keeps the personal stuff personal.

How content is made

Fresh packs come from a daily collection of trending and evergreen images, understood and cut into clean 512px stickers with short, send-ready captions, then checked for quality before they reach Discover. We respect personality rights and remove reported content.
